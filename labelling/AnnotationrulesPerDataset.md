Rules that have been followed when labelling the different genes for each iteration of the dataset
The rules are additive and come with at least 1 example

# CoreSet and Validation Dataset
1. **Clear Gene-Chondrogenesis Relationship**

The relationship between the gene and chondrogenesis must be explicitly stated in the text. Do not rely on external knowledge or inference to determine relevance.

<u>Example</u>: *"In addition, caMsx2 overexpression induced Ihh (Indian hedgehog) expression in mouse primary chondrocytes."* caMsx2 is not considered a regulator

2. **Direct Relation to Chondrogenesis Only**

Only genes that are directly involved in chondrogenesis should be labeled as related. Associations with general proliferation indicators should not be considered unless it is specified that is chondrogenesis related.

<u>Example</u>: *"IGFBP3-knockout MESCs culture acquired chondrocyte-like features, such as cell condensation and aggregation."* IGFBP3 is not considered a regulator

3. **Exclusion of Hypertrophy and Osteogenesis**

Genes related to hypertrophy or reduced osteogenesis should not be labeled as related to chondrogenesis.

<u>Example</u>: *"FOXC1 and FOXC2 regulate growth plate chondrocyte maturation towards hypertrophy in the embryonic mouse limb skeleton."* FOXC1 and FOXC2 are not considered regulators

4. **Cross-Species Consideration**

Information from all species is valid and should be taken into account when labeling.

<u>Example</u>: *"Importantly, TET1 inhibition in vivo in late stages of a mouse model of OA led to increased cartilage regeneration."* TET1 is considered a regulator

5. **Cell types Consideration**

We will consider all types of cells-lines for the search of chondorgenesis drivers

<u>Example</u>: *"In Conclusion, our data implies that miR-140 is a potent chondrogenic differentiation inducer for iPSCs and also, we have showed increasing chondrogenic differentiation by using overexpression of miR-140 and TGFb3."* miR-140 and TGFb3 are considered regulators

6. **All biomolecules Considered**

We will be considering a less strict definition of gene. This means that protein coding and non coding genes will be considered, in addition to moleculares as microRNAs

<u>Example</u>: *"Knockdown of miR-145 promoted chondrogenesis and inhibited hypertrophy differentiation in RMCs."*

7. **Bio markers relations**

We will be considering drivers related to the expression of chondrogenesis biomarkers such as ACAN, SOX9, etc only if there is another specifications in the text that they are specific chondrogenesis markers. We will not consider the genes drivers if the already known biomarkers are by themself.
If in the text only biomarkers are shown as proof, the biomarker proff box needs to be selected  when labelling the text

<u>Example</u>: *"Furthermore, Arid5a physically interacted with Sox9 in nuclei and up-regulated the chondrocyte-specific action of Sox9."* Arid5a is considered a regulator

8. **Mentioning of cartilage associated diseases**

We will not consider markers of diseases related to chondrogenesis. Only because the disease is associated to chondrogenesis, we will not take it as a driver of this process.

<u>Example</u>: *"Inhibiting the expression of CDKN1A can significantly suppress the differentiation of OA chondrocytes."* CDKN1A is not considered a regulator

9. **Functional Role vs Marker Role**

Only label genes that are described as having a direct functional or regulatory role in chondrogenesis. If a gene is mentioned only as a marker or indicator of chondrogenesis, do not label it as directly involved.

<u>Example</u>: *"Genome-wide mRNA expression analysis of the midpalatal suture tissue revealed that MLL4 is essential for the timely expression of major cartilage development genes, such as Col2a1 and Acan, at birth."* MLL4 is considered a regulator but not Col2a1 nor ACAN

# AL Iteration 1 
1. **Clear Gene-Chondrogenesis Relationship**

The relationship between the gene and chondrogenesis must be explicitly stated in the text. Do not rely on external knowledge or inference to determine relevance.

<u>Example</u>: *"In addition, caMsx2 overexpression induced Ihh (Indian hedgehog) expression in mouse primary chondrocytes."* caMsx2 is not considered a regulator

2. **Direct Relation to Chondrogenesis Only**

Only genes that are directly involved in chondrogenesis (or chondrogenesis potential) should be labeled as related. Associations with general proliferation indicators should not be considered unless it is specified that is chondrogenesis related.

<u>Example</u>: *"IGFBP3-knockout MESCs culture acquired chondrocyte-like features, such as cell condensation and aggregation."* IGFBP3 is not considered a regulator

3. **Modal verbs**

Genes which proof uses modal verbs such as "may","might", or "could", are considered speculative and should not be classified as evidence of chondrogenesis

<u>Example</u>: *"Differential mRNA expression of TGF-beta1 was found during all passages, which suggests that this growth factor might be involved in chondrocyte."* TGF-beta1 is not considered a regulator

4. **Negative evidence**

Do NOT consider negative evidence. If a gene "supports another process" and does not influence chondrogenesis directly, it is not counted as being involved in chondrogenesis.

<u>Example</u>: *"Knockdown of FOXA2 promoted osteogenic differentiation of bone-marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells while not altering chondrogenic potential"* FOXA2 is not considered to be a regulator

5. **Pathways and axis**

Genes that are part of chondrogenic pathways or axes should not be classified as drivers when mentioned as intermediates or downstream markers. In such cases, they serve as evidence (phenotypic or biomarker proof) that another gene acts as a regulator of chondrogenesis.

<u>Example</u>: *"Cytl1 exerted its chondrogenic effect via stimulation of Sox9 transcriptional activity"* Cytl1 is considered a regulator

6. **Exclusion of Hypertrophy and Osteogenesis**

Genes related to hypertrophy (also referred to as "terminal chondrogenesis", or "terminal maturation") or reduced osteogenesis should not be labeled as related to chondrogenesis.

<u>Example</u>: *"FOXC1 and FOXC2 regulate growth plate chondrocyte maturation towards hypertrophy in the embryonic mouse limb skeleton."* FOXC1 and FOXC2 are not considered regulators

7. **Exclusion of Cell Proliferation and Repair**

Genes related to only cell proliferation or cartilage repair should not be labeled as related to chondrogenesis.

<u>Example</u>: *"We found that silencing of miR-221 strongly enhanced in vivo cartilage repair compared to the control conditions."* miR-221 is not considered a regulator

8. **Cross-Species Consideration**

10. **Tri-lineage cells Consideration**

In this dataset, any sentence mentioning "tri-lineage cells", should be treated as indicating chondrocytes, osteoblasts and adipocytes, since our data acquirement was focused on chondrogenesis.

<u>Example</u>: *"We have shown that overexpression of Sox11 in rMSCs by lentivirus-mediated gene transfer leads to enhanced tri-lineage differentiation and accelerated bone formation in fracture model of rats."* Sox11 is considered a regulator
